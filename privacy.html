✳ A CLEAR LITTLE NOTE

Your visits.
Your privacy.

This site uses privacy-conscious aggregate analytics to understand how many people visit. It does not use advertising trackers.

What is counted

When the home page loads, your browser sends a small request to our Cloudflare analytics endpoint. Cloudflare receives your IP address as network metadata; the function immediately uses a secret-keyed hash of it to count approximate unique visitors and page visits. The original IP address is not written to this site’s analytics database, and individual addresses are not shown in the admin dashboard.

How long it stays

The IP-derived visitor identifier is kept for up to 90 days so the dashboard can avoid counting the same address more than once per day. Expired identifiers are removed when analytics next runs. Daily totals remain after those identifiers expire. Counts are approximate: people sharing a network may appear as one visitor, and changing networks can appear as a new visitor.

Your choice

No analytics cookies are set. The site skips analytics when your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control. The site’s hosting provider also processes requests to deliver the pages.

Analytics are used only to show aggregate visit counts to the site owner.
